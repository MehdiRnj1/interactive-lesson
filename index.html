Spelling lab and tweets

Grammar

LEARN THIS! Spelling: -ing forms

a
Most verbs: add -ing to the infinitive.
eat → eating   go → going
b
Verbs ending in -e: replace -e with -ing.
dance → dancing   phone → phoning
c
One vowel + one consonant: double the consonant.
chat → chatting   stop → stopping
